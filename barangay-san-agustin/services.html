Frontline Services

The Barangay Hall issues the following documents and provides community services. Please bring a valid ID and the listed requirements.

Service / DocumentPurposeRequirementsFee*
Barangay Clearance Employment, business, legal, and other transactions Valid ID; proof of residency; Cedula (Community Tax Certificate) ₱ 50.00
Certificate of Residency Proof of being a bona fide resident Valid ID; minimum 6 months residency ₱ 30.00
Certificate of Indigency Medical, educational, or financial assistance Valid ID; proof of residency Free
Business Permit Endorsement Barangay clearance for new/renewing businesses DTI/SEC registration; lease/title; valid ID Varies by business
Certificate of Good Moral Character School, scholarship, or employment requirement Valid ID; proof of residency ₱ 30.00
Barangay Blotter / Incident Report Recording of incidents and complaints Valid ID; details of the incident Free
Katarungang Pambarangay Amicable settlement of disputes (Lupong Tagapamayapa) Written or verbal complaint; valid ID Free

*Fees are indicative placeholders. Actual fees are set by the Sangguniang Barangay ordinance. Confirm at the Barangay Hall.

Community Programs

Barangay Health Station

Basic health consultations, immunization, prenatal care, and referrals to the Iba Rural Health Unit.

Peace & Order

Barangay Tanod patrols, BPAT, and the Barangay Public Safety program for a secure community.

Disaster Preparedness

BDRRMC operations, evacuation planning, and early warning for floods and typhoons.

Youth & SK Programs

Sports, leadership, and educational activities led by the Sangguniang Kabataan.

Senior Citizens & PWD

Assistance, ID processing, and welfare programs for senior citizens and persons with disabilities.

Cleanliness & Environment

Solid waste management, coastal clean-up drives, and tree-growing activities.

Office Hours

The Barangay Hall is open for frontline transactions:

  • Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Saturday: 8:00 AM – 12:00 NN (skeletal staff)
  • Sunday & Holidays: Closed (Barangay Tanod on duty 24/7)

Under the Ease of Doing Business Act (R.A. 11032), simple transactions are processed within the day. For assistance, please approach the front desk or call the barangay hotline.

How to Request a Document

  1. Proceed to the Barangay Hall front desk.
  2. State your purpose and present a valid ID.
  3. Fill out the request form and submit requirements.
  4. Pay the corresponding fee (if any) to the Treasurer.
  5. Claim your document upon release.
