About Our Barangay

Getting to know Barangay San Agustin — its people, history, and aspirations.

Overview

Barangay San Agustin is one of the constituent barangays of the Municipality of Iba, the capital town of the Province of Zambales in Central Luzon (Region III). Iba lies along the coast of the West Philippine Sea and is bounded by the Zambales Mountain Range to the east.

Like the rest of Iba, the barangay enjoys a tropical climate with a pronounced dry and wet season. Residents derive their livelihood mainly from agriculture (rice and vegetables), fishing, small enterprises, and employment in the town center where provincial and municipal offices are located.

Brief History

The barangay traces its name to its devotion to San Agustin (St. Augustine). Through the years, what began as a small cluster of farming and fishing households grew into an organized community with its own barangay government, recognized as a basic political unit under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160).

The Community Today

Barangay San Agustin is home to a close-knit population that values bayanihan, faith, and resilience. The barangay government continues to invest in basic infrastructure, disaster preparedness, health, peace and order, and youth and senior citizen programs to uplift the quality of life of every resident.
